re Mecca project...

the whole project is more about the ways in which we stay sane or don’t stay sane, 
than anything else. It’s about identity; identity which is the same as  personal 
cultural practice, the perfect example being the collecting of lucky charms , 
habits and rituals. The creation of a self, a personality. Gambling and bingo 
seems to be about the erosion of the personal that is strong, addiction is a 
kind of weakness, a need to escape one’s individuality, or self more to the point. 
Drinking, gambling and religion, you join a club, a group of people who all do that. 
The addiction is fraught with paradox. It brings you closer to God sometimes, 
Drugs reveal miraculous sensations etc, so does evry-aholism; the buzz of controlling; 
so  does gambling; control the machines. 

At the heart of the project lies these paradoxes. Addiction is one extreme of comfort. 
Something desirable taken to extreme.
