GEORGE: Oh yeah, you'll always find more....we had them come round one time .....I knew the manager was at it , but I didn't know what he was doing, I couldn't suss how he was doing it....because he'd go down the road, and he says, "Coming down the betting shop, George?" and I'd say "Ahh no....I'm fed up with it." and he"d say "Oh, you can't lose if you bet like this!" .
