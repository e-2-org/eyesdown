TALKING MECCA



Did you do any of the questionnaires before ?
Have you seen any of the images or projections around the building ?
Which images?  where were they?
What did you think about them, did they remind you of anything?
How long have you been coming to Bingo?
Why did you start coming to Bingo and has it changed much since you first started coming?
What are your memories of the State?
Why do you come to Bingo? Do you come to win, for a social time, or both?
Do you come with friends?  do you meet friends here?
What would you be doing if you didn’t come to Bingo?
Do you ever play Bingo anywhere else, like the church hall, local pub, 
 and how does that compare to Bingo here?
When and how often do you play ?
How many books do you play at a time?
Have you ever won at Bingo or any other games?
What did it feel like to have a big win?
What did you spend your prize money on?
Do you play the lottery, fruit machines, horses, dogs, casinos?
Are you superstitious? do you believe in luck? fate?
Do you have any lucky charms? Where did you get them from?
Do you have any lucky numbers? How did you choose them?
What about a lucky pen, place to sit, or rituals?
Do you think that winning happens by chance or is fated?
Can we make our own “luck”?
What would you say you believe in?
Are you religious, do you believe in God?
When we first came to Bingo we thought it was a bit like church in a way : the caller, 
  the congregation, the building itself, eyes down etc.  Do you think there’s a connection?
Do you believe in miracles? If you could have one miracle, what would it be?
Have you ever dreamt about winning ?
What do you think about The State, the decor?
Why do you think Bingo is less busy, less  popular now than it was 20 or 30 years ago?
What do you think about these ad campaigns trying to encourage new/younger people to Bingo?
