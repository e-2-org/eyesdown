PAT: ... Well, this man on the corner was in the process of putting his sail down....the mast down.....as we swung around, and the boat started going over towards him. And just as this mast come down, it just come straight through the front window of the boat, straight out the other......and there was a television as well.....very thickly, thickly quilted inside , and it went straight through the quilting. But it didn't go out the other end of the boat. Of course, we saw this happening, and I still see it today in slow motion. It was as if we see this huge .....I mean, masts only look that......until they're coming towards you........and it pierced , and then it come through, and it was like a spider's web, all in slow motion, a spider's web where the mast come through. Well, we couldn't go any further, it was a huge big panoramic window at the front, you know. So, of course everybody had their cameras out, there were all these Japanese tourists, all running to the side taking pictures, and we were trying to hide and draw the curtains, you know. (All laugh) The pilot phoned from the boatyard........that's when it was, it was at the time of the Falklands, because every day we's limp home wi' another bit missing off the boat - and it was a whole corner missing off the front - every day there was something.....and someone would say "You've had a good day today. I'd like to see the other one!" But, the pilot came, and of course he had to tie us up and we had to stay there over night because he had to send someone from base, sort of thing, to put all new windows in and everything. And then the next morning, he said "I'll take you out this little inlet, back into the Broads, and turn you to go up." So, we says ok then, which he did . And he took us out , and as we got to the corner he jumped off. And he says "Now turn right, and go back up the Broads". There was a big huge boat, moored just on the other side, and of course we just went straight into that ! (laughs) The kids, they used to go up on the sun deck, and they used to take their pillows, and their blankets, and then come down for something to eat, forget they were up there......and we'd look back, and all the pillows and blankets were floating back .......It was lucky this holiday was completely insured by Top Rank, you know? But we eventually made it back to where we started from......
