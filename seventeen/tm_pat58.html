KAREN: What, they overturned?

PAT: Mm. It was awful! And I'll always remember this place - it was called Wroxham - and we were going down towards it, and there was this little olde worlde bridge - little tiny bridge - Oh, goodness! Anyway, as we got near to the bridge, the boats from the other side were coming through, all lined up this side, and of course, we couldn't stop, see? And the kids had got friends - young boys on this other boat - and we were shouting to them, as we're trying to get near them "Will you catch our rope to stop us?" No, they wouldn't, sort of thing. So, it was my fault because I was steering.....as I went to go under the bridge, the boat sort of turned sideways, and we were stuck across the way, see? And of course by this time acrowd had gathered on the bridge at the one side, and the boat had started to go through the bridge sideways, just scraping each side - scrape, scrape , scrape. And it must have been.....the bridge was not even ten feet long, and by the time we'd scraped through sideways, they'd all come over the bridge to the other side . Oh! you've never seen a mess like it! And we thought "What are we going to do?"Anyway, somebody grabbed us from the left hand side, so we tied up there, and we stayed in Wroxham that night. We thought, "What are we going to do? How do we get back up again?" you know. But eventually, the next morning, we did, and one of the kids took over, and they were a lot better than us, sort of thing. And we went up and up and up and up andup........and it was Potter Higham, and, seemingly you can only go so far, and you must get - oh, I forget his name - the pilot. He must take you through that bridge, you see? So, we got up there, and there was lots and lots of boats parked each side of these huge houses with big gardens, and their little boats on the water.....well they were just not.......we used to look back and see them all bobbing off back down the Broads, and everything. You know, this was just like bloomin' .....what was it called in those days?......Candid Camera, you know - someone has to be filming this. But, as we neared Potter Higham, we saw the bridge, and we thought "Ohh, we'll never get through!". So, I can't remember if it was me, or was it Jill, or Jackie......? One of them swerved the boat around, and the bridge was ahead, and there were lots and lots of yachts there, and all the sails down, sort of thing.
