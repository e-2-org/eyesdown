TALKING MECCA
Conversations at The State Mecca bingo Hall,Kilburn. June 1999.


? 
In conversation with Karen Stripp and Karyn Narvey.




?: I was here last night, but I missed yesterday afternoon. What films did you show? The old film of this place?

KAREN: That was on a television, here.....

?: What was it, a video?

KAREN: That was the video of when they opened it in the thirties. And then, inside we had a little film which was all bits and pieces of....we asked people things, like what they’re superstitious about, what ther’ve spent their money on when they’ve had a big win - things like that.

?: I’ve had a hundred pound in thirty years!

KAREN: A hundred pounds? Is that all? That’s terrible.

?: No, why I wanted to query was - my mum and dad was at the opening of this. And I wanted to know if there was any chance of getting a video, or something like that, like.

KAREN: What, a copy of it, you mean? I don’t know......

?: I thought it was on last night as well, you see. I came here last night......

KAREN: No......would a lot of people be interested in seeing it, do you think?

?: Oh, good God, yes! My mum and dad’s dead now, like, but it was beautiful - lovely waterfall in here.....

KAREN: A waterfall? Whereabouts?

?: I don’t know, I’m not quite sure about it. But she said it was beautiful, and piles of people here, you know - all different stars - it was really lovely. But I would like to.....

KAREN: You’d love to see it. Well,we can ask if maybe they can show it another time. Because, if a lot of people would be interested....

?: Oh, I think there would be quite a few. I thought, Oh God, it would be lovely to see my mum and dad. ‘Cos they were young then, like, you know...... Ok, then, thanks.....
