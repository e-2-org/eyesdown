KAREN: He'd been selling cigarettes as well.....!

GEORGE: You know, he'd been putting empty packets in place of.....so they thought,"Oh, the machine's full up, there won't be any money to collect" but it was full up with empty packets !

ANNA: And that was that - gone, never seen nor heard of again?

GEORGE: There was another one....I don't know how he didn't get in the papers for it. He came to the Carlton Rooms once - I was there donkeys years ago - he appeared there once, "visiting management " or something, and he thought he was a showman, you know. Fat bloke, only about twenty-two or something like that I suppose. And he'd say,"Hallo George!" and all this. Now I don't know him from Adam, and I don't really like all that kind of thing. "I'll call the start of this session, just for a few games, get me hand in......Good evening Ladies and Gents! I'm so-and-so...!" And all this lark.....and they went (groan) - you know how some of them are - and he's going on, all bombastic, "I'll leave the rest to George - go on, George...." he'd swank about the hall, having a drink and that. I thought, "what sort of man's this?" And he had this...I don't know if it was his wife, or girlfriend, always on his shoulder, like, wherever he went. And, anyway, saw no more of him, and then we found out there was....MECCA clubs - that's the old MECCA, we had over a hundred clubs, we were the biggest in Bingo - there wereMecca clubs being held up at gunpoint by two or three people. Anyway, they sussed out in the end somehow it was this bloke, and he was doing armed holdups - he was a nutcase, he must've seen Bonnie and Clyde or something and gone berserk! And...he'd know the layout of the club , and then he started doing clubs where they knew him ! They knew his face!

KAREN: NO ! Didn't he try and disguise himself? Put a stocking over his head?

GEORGE: No.

ANNA: That's amazing! He must've been living out a fantasy.....
