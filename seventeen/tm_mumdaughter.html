TALKING MECCA
Conversation s at The State Mecca Bingo Hall, Kilburn. June 1999.



Un-namedMOTHER andDAUGHTER.
In conversation with Karen Stripp and Justine Marley.




KAREN: So, you said you hadn’t seen any of the projections around the building? 

DAUGHTER: No, we ain’t been for a while, have we?

MUM: No.

KAREN: Right. So do you come quite regularly?

DAUGHTER: We used to. We used to come , like, every Tuesday, every Sunday. We used to come quite regular, didn’t we?

MUM: Yes. It depends on the caller as well.

DAUGHTER: Yeah, it depends on the caller.

MUM: Some can be.....

DAUGHTER: Hmm, yeah, they  put you to sleep. They really can put you to sleep!

MUM: I have - I’ve nodded off a couple of times, when a certain fella’s calling.....

(All laugh)

JUSTINE: Really? When they’ve been calling?

MUM: Yeah, I have. It’s just.......

DAUGHTER: There’s one geezer in here called George.....

KAREN: Yes, we know George....

DAUGHTER: He’s just.....he’s so boring! 

MUM: (Laughs) That’s the one - George.

JUSTINE: Did you actually fall asleep? Or you nearly fell asleep?

DAUGHTER: No, she was.....falling asleep!

MUM: I think his nickname’s "The Undertaker"!

KAREN: (Laughs) Is it?

DAUGHTER: Yeah...

KAREN: I haven’t heard that before........does he know?

DAUGHTER: No...

JUSTINE: So how often did you go to Bingo? You said you used to go Tuesdays and Sundays....

DAUGHTER: We used to go really regularly, didn’t we, at one point?  ‘Cos I was on this, like, lucky streak. Every time I’d go, I was winning, or just winning my money back....and then.....that was it.

KAREN: And then it went?

DAUGHTER: Yeah. 

MUM: My husband and I used to argue about  Bingo when these were small. (Laughs)

JUSTINE: About who was going to go, and who was going to look after the kids?

MUM: Yes! 

JUSTINE: Did you both compromise in the end, or.....?

MUM: No, I usually went! There was one particular night when we went to Bingo.....I went to Bingo and I’d won five times in that night. And he came down for the late session, and he won twice! 

KAREN: You won five times in one night?

MUM: Yeah!

DAUGHTER: It wasn’t much money......

KAREN: But still.....

DAUGHTER: See, if I won once, I couldn’t ....I wouldn’t shout again, ‘cos I’d think everyone was, like, hating me......

KAREN: You’re mad!

JUSTINE: Wouldn’t you?

DAUGHTER: I probably would, but I’d probably say to Mum to shout.......

KAREN: You’d be embarrassed....

DAUGHTER: Yeah.

MUM: I’d shout, I don’t care!

JUSTINE: We had a game at....we actually put on a whole Bingo game at Camden Arts Centre back in April, and one of the women that works here - Gabby  - came. Now she doesn’t play Bingo,she works here. She doesn’t  get Bingo, she doesn’t get it at all, she’s not into it - is she? - as a game. And she won about five, six times. And all the people that come to play Bingo here, they were there, and they....

DAUGHTER: (Laughs) "Fix!"

JUSTINE: ....and I thought: "Good on her!", you know? Because she never plays....beginners luck. But she said afterwards it was getting to point  where it was embarrassing.

KAREN: She was embarrassed, bless her!

JUSTINE: I would definately  call. I would definately  shout if I won.....

DAUGHTER: But say if you won a big lot and then you win it again ......?

KAREN: But do you....I’ve noticed that people are quite  often so cool about it.

MUM: Oh yes!

KAREN: I mean, I was at the beacon one night, and there was a woman won three times in a night. The chance of that happening.....I think it was aFriday night, or a Saturday  night - it was packed! I’ve never won anything there.....I’ve won on the cash bingo, and that’s it - and she won about a couple of thousand pounds twice, and then, like, five hundred pounds. It was ridiculous!  It was big money, and she was so cool......

DAUGHTER: I’d want to mug her or something, on the way out!

MUM: Show a bit of excitement!  D’you know what I mean?

DAUGHTER: Yeah...I don’t like that. I don’t like that. No "thankyou", or....

MUM: They don’t really want it......

JUSTINE: Or they seem like they don’t....

MUM: It’s like these people who win the Lottery, and they say they don’t want to change  their lives, and they don’t want to give up work.....why bother?

DAUGHTER: You would, though. You would.

JUSTINE: It’s funny, actually, because  I was up at the newspaper archive  in......wherever it is.....somewhere over that way.......and there was loads of stuff, I was doing loads of research into actual Lottery winners and newspaper articles,  and reading about all the different stories, and, I mean, you get quite involved. But in a lot of them they were saying that they didn’t want to change  their lives, and a lot of people were saying that when they’d won the Lottery, they really wished they’d never won it and stuff.......

DAUGHTER: Yeah..’cos it does - money really changes people. 

JUSTINE: ......and they said their friends walk across on the other side of the street because they don’t know how to speak to them anymore......it’s a shame, isn’t it?

MUM: But, I find it silly, because if you’re lucky enough to win it, there’s so many good things you can do with it. If you don’t want it, there’s so many  different charities, or....all different things.

KAREN: Yes. So when you’ve won, is there anything in particular that  you’ve spent your money on?

DAUGHTER: When ever I’ve had a win, I’ve always got a telephone bill, or something to pay. Any time I win big, I’ve got to pay.......like Christmas, the time I won four hundred pound, it was Christmas the next week, so I went out and I splashed out on all nice  presents. And I thought, I should’ve kept some money for myself......

KAREN: There’s always something to pay for isn’t there? 

DAUGHTER: ....yeah...but, I didn’t have it to begin with, so......

JUSTINE: It’s nice though, isn’t it, to be able to go out and say "I’ve got plenty, here, I don’t have to worry, I just buy what I want to get."?

MUM: Do you ever find that, when you haven’t any money with you, or whatever it is, you want something so badly......

DAUGHTER: .....and when you’ve got the money, you don’t want it.

MUM: .....you don’t really want it any more!

JUSTINE: Yes, I’m exactly  like that. I’m always needing clothes, or something and something and something......and then when I’ve got the money I just.......I don’t want to spend it ....it’s too easy, it’s not a challenge anymore.

MUM: Yes...you’ve gone off the idea.

KAREN: So what......if you had a big win, what would you spend it on?

(Pause)

MUM: A holiday.

DAUGHTER: Holiday, yeah.

JUSTINE: Where?

DAUGHTER: Disneyland.

JUSTINE: Which? Where?

DAUGHTER: Well, we’ve been to America - Florida....

MUM: We’ve been to the Paris one....

KAREN: In Florida. Not California?

JUSTINE: Isn’t that Disneyworld?

KAREN: Disneyland’s in California.

DAUGHTER: Yeah. Disneyland. You’d go to the one in Florida, wouldn’t you - Disneyworld ? .....It’s like we’re really going  now, isn’t it? (all laugh)

JUSTINE: Well, you’ve got to dream  it, haven’t you ?

DAUGHTER: .....erm, I don’t know.....I’d probably, like, hire out a limo for the night....and go Stringfellows and all that.

JUSTINE: Would you? Go to Stringfellows?

DAUGHTER: I would. I’d just hit the West End, treat all my friends to a night out.....

MUM: We were just talking about that, coming up the road, weren’t  we?

DAUGHTER: Yeah.

JUSTINE: Ok, but say you won....like.....

DAUGHTER: Mega...loadsamoney.....! 

JUSTINE: .......big bucks.....?

DAUGHTER: I’d go....go away.

JUSTINE: What, leave England?

DAUGHTER: No...just for a holiday. I’d probably go America, see all my friends over there, and then come back.

JUSTINE: And then what would you do?

DAUGHTER: Maybe  then go Ibiza. Then come back.

JUSTINE: So, you’d just go on holidays all the time? 

DAUGHTER: Probably, yeah.

MUM: America, and Australia.

KAREN: Do you do the Lottery or anything?

MUM: Yes.

KAREN: Do you do anything else, like ......dogs, or horses...?

DAUGHTER: Fourty-niners, Irish Lottery.....everything! (Laughs)

KAREN: Do you?  The Irish Lottery’s good.....

JUSTINE: What’s the Fourty-niners ?

MUM: It’s a daily thing....

DAUGHTER: It’s the betting shop lottery. It’s like the National Lottery, but.....

KAREN: You get more chances as well, don’t you?  You do with the Irish Lottery.....

MUM: You do the Irish Lottery, right? For a pound, you stake on the English Lottery, you get three numbers, you get ten pound, yeah? You stake  a pound on the Irish Lottery, you get three numbers, you get four hundred and twenty one pound! Which would you bet on?

JUSTINE: Well, Irish. Of course.

MUM: I’ve got it already. I’ve got it before, three times!

DAUGHTER: She’s very lucky.....very lucky with it.

KAREN: Do you do Magic Numbers as well?

MUM: No, I don’t like that one.

DAUGHTER: One time she’d done it - yeah? - she’d forgotten that she’d done it in Ladbrokes,and went and done it in Corals.....and both of ‘em come up!

KAREN: No! So you got it twice?

MUM: Yes!

KAREN: That’s really good!

DAUGHTER: Lucky.....

JUSTINE: Do you use the same numbers all the time?

MUM: I do, yes.

KAREN: So...lucky numbers......?

MUM: Birthdays, things like that. The grandchildren’s birthdays.....

JUSTINE: Can I.....I don’t have to write them down but can I ask you what those numbers are? Just out of interest....

MUM: Yes. They’re : 1,11,22;  1,11,21;  22,8,35. And 10,16,21.

JUSTINE: 22 and 11 are both really interesting numbers. What other numbers?

KAREN: A lot of people  have 11, actually.

MUM: 8. 11 is a good banking number. Err.....35’s a good one.....and 10,16.....

JUSTINE: 35...3 and 5 adds up to 8 as well.

DAUGHTER: It does, doesn’t it?

JUSTINE: Now these are good because they’re both the same.....

DAUGHTER: See, you know on the back of them they’ve  got like a a thing to show you how to do it - they’ve got numbers  - and she does them numbers on the back of the slip, and they’ve come up.

MUM: It’s a good idea, especially on the....there’s a 49ers bingo slip, right? And at the back it’s got the.....illustration of........

KAREN: That’s right, it shows you.

MUM: It’s just....it’s worth it, even if it’s for a pound or fifty pence. Just put ‘em down - you never know!

KAREN: I’ve done Magic Numbers, and the thing is with the Magic  Numbers is that people think it’s really easy - if you just have six numbers, you’ll get it. But the chances are so small , because they always go for the big prize. If you pick a lot of numbers, the chances  of you winning ten quid are  actually much bigger. I’ve won a tenner, twenty, quite often like that. You just have to think small, and you can just build it up.....

DAUGHTER: See, I don’t do that, because they do it every hour, don’t they?

KAREN: Yes, I got fed up with it.

DAUGHTER: If you’re sitting there, every hour, it’s going to cost you a lot of money.

KAREN: And do you do the horses or dogs as well? 

DAUGHTER: I do the horses ......... 

MUM: And that Thunderball, or whatever they call it.......I did that one, that was twenty, and that was thirteen.....and it came in....

DAUGHTER: I always do three name horses.

KAREN: What, three words, in the name?  

DAUGHTER: Yeah. They always win. They always win! One time, I picked a horse, and I didn’t do it. And I wrote it out on the bet......and, my sister works in the betting shop - she’s a manager at Ladbrokes.....I done one pound each way double....I went ‘I’m A Dreamer’, and that ‘Cash Box’, or something like that.....and I didn’t put it on. And ‘I’m A Dreamer’ won at 50/1 and the other one came in at 33/1! And my sister rang me saying "Did you do your horse?".....I was gutted!

KAREN: I know....I do the Placepot quite a lot, and I always narrow them down, thinking I can’t put another selection there because  it costs too much, and the one I’ve missed out always comes in, you know for, like....big money!

DAUGHTER: Oh, no! (Laughs)  See, I even betted on ‘Stars In Your Eyes’! Did you bet on that?

KAREN: No!

DAUGHTER: You can bet on who’s going to win ‘Stars In Your Eyes’! (Both Laugh)

JUSTINE: I played the Lottery when it came out , I played it all the time.....and I’ve done it a few times since, and I’ve never even got one number on it. So I just don’t bother now......

MUM: Yes.....Well, the two times I’ve done the Thunderball, I got two balls.

JUSTINE: Which means what?That you win?

MUM: Yes, you win. It’s only a tenner.

JUSTINE: That’s ten pounds on a ticket?

MUM: No, I placed two pound , and I won a tenner.

JUSTINE: Well....that’s alright.

MUM: Yes, but if those two other numbers had come up, I’d have had a couple of hundred pound.

JUSTINE: So, if you get all your five numbers, that’s a couple of hundred quid?

MUM: Oh no, if you get all five, you get maybe two hundred and fifty thousand or something.

JUSTINE: Oh, right. Big money. That’s not the Irish Lottery though, is it?

MUM: That’s the English.....

JUSTINE: How often do they do the Irish?

MUM: They do it twice a week, Wednesday and Saturday.

JUSTINE: The same?

MUM: Yeah.

JUSTINE: Oh, right. Do you have to go to the bookies to do it?

MUM: Yeah. 

JUSTINE: Do you have any lucky numbers. Do you have, like, a lucky number? Like  you said eight....

DAUGHTER: What’s your luckiest number?

JUSTINE: You said eight was a good banking number....

MUM: Eight is a good banking number.

JUSTINE: What does that mean: a banking number?

MUM: It comes out a lot.

JUSTINE: Right. Would you say that’s a lucky number for you, then? 

MUM: 11 and 22.

KAREN: So, have you got lucky charms  or anything like that?

DAUGHTER: I used to come here every week with my lucky Bingo board - that was from Ireland - and then a leprechaun.......

MUM: What  time’s it starting?

DAUGHTER: Oh, we’d better go, it’s starting in a minute.

JUSTINE: Well...whatever they say....

KAREN: Not ‘Good Luck’ - don’t say that!

JUSTINE: No, I’m not....(all laugh).
