TALKING MECCA
Conversations at The State Mecca Bingo Hall, Kilburn. 1999.



IVY, a player.
In conversation with Karen Stripp, Anna Best, Karyn Narvey. 


IVY: If people didn’t play Bingo, they would’t have understood the film. ‘Cos, when we was coming down yesterday, there was an elderly couple here. And we said, "What did you think of the film?". She turned round and she said "Absolutely rubbish!". And I said "Well, do you play Bingo?". She says "No." You know, if you don’t play Bingo, then they wouldn’t understand all the things, you know, like.....the ladder, you know? Going under the ladder......I thought it was good. Very well put together.

ANNA: That’s very nice to hear.

IVY: It was different, yes.

ANNA: What about the Wurlitzer?

IVY: Oh, yes - fantastic, it really is. The thing is that it still plays so well, after all this time, you know?

ANNA: Have you heard it before?

IVY: Oh, yes, a couple of times. 

KAREN: So did any one else say anything about it? Did you overhear anybody commenting.....?

IVY: I haven’t spoke...no, I haven’t spoke to anybody else since. But .....we’ll probably hear rumours, as we go along, you know......what they think.

ANNA: You’ll hear rumours as you go along.....

IVY: Yes, you know - they’ll come and say "Was you here?" or they’ll say "It wasn’t this...." or "It wasn’t that....". You know.

KAREN: Yes......so there were things in the film that you sort of recognised..?

IVY: Oh, of course, yes! If you’re playing Bingo, then....you know, you understood it.

ANNA: Did you see any of the other projections?

IVY: Yes.

ANNA: Have you noticed them?

IVY: Yes

ANNA: Throughout June?

IVY: Yes, that’s right.

KAREN: Yes. What was it you said that you’d seen? Was it the black cat that you’d seen? No.....

IVY: No....the fella doing up his shoelaces.....

KAREN: That was Anna doing up her shoelaces!

(All Laugh)

IVY: Was it? Oh. (Laughs). Well, you can’t see....you can only see the thing....you know, so it could be.....

ANNA: There it is!

(All laugh as ‘New Boots’ image appears)

KAREN: It was there for a second.....

IVY: Yeah.....that was surprising....

KARYN: Did you enjoy the music? You know, the actual music itself - did it bring back any memories at all?

IVY: Oh yes, some of the old tunes. Wasn’t too sure about the organ when you was calling Bingo, because that was a bit......

ANNA: That was quite weird wasn’t it? 

IVY: Yes. 

ANNA: But did you know it was a song called ‘Sally’....did you recognise it?

KAREN: Gracie Fields....

IVY: Oh, yeah, I recognised the song.

ANNA: I felt like it was almost like...you heard the first bit of the song, and then it stopped, and you were calling. Then you heard the next bit. There were a couple of times when it felt like it was carrying on, and then other times when it just wasn’t.

IVY: It didn’t, that’s right, yes. Yes, it was different. No, it was really interesting. So, are you here for long now or are you going on?

KAREN: Here for another day, just talking to people, if they want to talk about what they thought about yesterday.....

ANNA: We’ll be around for another couple of days after that.....I mean, the projections are finishing on Wednesday, so that’s the last day. And then we’ve got to be in on Thursday to give all the equipment back.....and then, you probably won’t see us around for a while. ‘Cos we’re going to try to gather all the...well, you probably explained....

KAREN: The sort of booklet....

ANNA: And when we’ve done the  booklet, we should have something here, I think - some kind of event, again, to say "Here it is..."

IVY: Yeah, to say that it’s finished.....

ANNA: And then everyone can come and get one, take one away, and all that stuff.....

IVY: Of course, read it and see.

KAREN: That would be good....

IVY: It would, definately.

ANNA: I think it will take a while. We’ve got a lot of material......

(PAUSE)

KAREN: We’re waiting for the boots, now.....

ANNA: You have to be quick....I think that’s really interesting what you were saying about having to play Bingo to understand the film. It’s exactly what we were talking about earlier. Because people found it really obscure....they were like, random. My flatmate was just saying "Talk about the Random Number Generator......I think you’re a random image generator!" 

(Laughter)

ANNA: Because it was really random, wasn’t it?

KAREN: Yes...they couldn’t make a connection. I was wondering if anyone would recognise their Bingo books, actually. Because some of them are really.....kind of noticeable. They’re like signatures, almost.

IVY: They are, yeah - the way some of them mark them.

KAREN: I was wondering if someone was going to come and say "You’ve used one of my books!"

ANNA: What do you do? Do you dab? 

IVY: No, I just put a line - black pen - just one line.

KAREN: You always use a black pen, don’t you?

IVY: Yes, I like black pens.

KAREN: I’ve forgotten your name. I’m really sorry...

IVY: Ivy.

KAREN: Ivy - that’s right! I’ve got you in my ‘library’. Ivy - she’s in there!

(Laughter)

KAREN: You're there for Eternity, now...!
