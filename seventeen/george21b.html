ANNA: Can you throw them away? Or are you meant to return them?

GEORGE: You must have them on the premises for a certain amount of time. In a small club, you probably don't have to send them to Head Office or anything, you know, you can just keep them in sacks of rubbish. Then if the Gaming Board ever did bother to call on this grotty little place , they wouldn't want to sort through all that... and, according to him, there was hundreds and hundreds of rejected books, that didn't really exist. So they'd been sold, and he'd had the money, so it didn't go on the prize money board........see how it works? It was the simplest thing.... he was rolling in it. But he left there...I don't know what happenened there, but anyway....

ANNA: How do you know he committed suicide?

GEORGE: He was erm......he had this girlfriend he always lived with, and'cos I suppose it must be awkward if you've only got one leg, sort of.....getting a chat up line ... .(All Laugh)...old Hoppy.......it'd be hard getting a new bird....so he was really attached to this bird even though they weren't married.....he was really attached to this bird, that he....like....he lived for her, sort of thing. And er...she dumped him. A few weeks later we heard he'd killed himself. But nowadays I don't think there's as much skullduggery and that.
