KAREN: ...Indestructible....

GEORGE: Yeah. I think he thought he'd already been sussed, so he thought "Bugger it. Why cover up if I've been sussed?" and he was just doing it , going from club to club, and the alarm was out , you know,"If you see so-and-so..." and there was a picture of him, "....call the police immediately, and don't ...er...tackle'im in any way,'cos he carries an armed gun at all times."

ANNA: So when was that? How many years ago was that?

GEORGE: Ohh, years ago now. Fifteen or twenty. He went down...I forget how long....he went to prison. Raving mad!

ANNA: Robbing Bingo halls! How much money do you think....? I suppose there is quite a lot of money isn't there, on the premises in a day?

GEORGE: Yeah, he'd get thousands. He'd know when there was money there.

KAREN: And where it was, and...

GEORGE: Yeah...........Funny the way people turn....don't they?

ANNA: People are amazing, the things they do.

GEORGE: Another old trick is the booze. If they come and check your stock of booze, and find all the vodka and gin bottles are full of water . They've done that.

KAREN: Oh God, I bet...
