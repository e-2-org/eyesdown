Questions for 'Playtime'

Amy Plant and Anna Best - questions for 'Playtime' discussion

what did you think about the game, as a parallel space to life? And how does this relate to risk taking in art, gambling or religion?

Can you say something about the time and space designated by games?

Is the notion of participation important? why bring participation into Art?

Does the artist have a right to play with the viewer? How could art become a fair game?

Are games, art and religion considered to varying degrees to be either educational or merely a way to kill time?

Has accessibility got anything to do with the reasons behind curating Game Show?

Why do you think Art has become so bereft of participation and of notions of daily performance?

What is the difference between a game or gambling and just playing (make believe etc)?
