PD Ouspensky
In Search of the Miraculous: Fragments of an Unknown Teaching

"when leaving Petersburg at the start of my journey I had said that I was going to "seek the miraculous". The "miraculous" is very difficult to define... I had come to the conclusion that there was no escape from this labyrinth of contradictions in which we live ...beyond the thin film of false reality there existed another reality from which, for some reason, something separated us. The "miraculous" was a penetration into this unknown reality."p.3

"Try to understand what I am saying. Everything is dependant on everything else, everything is connected, nothing is separate. Therefore everything is going in the only way it can go."p.22

"I remember that assertions of this kind made me indignant...people who had passed through any kind of extraordinary experiences and afterwards refused to explain them. They had sought the miraculous...they said"I have found it. But I cannot describe what I have found."p.260-261

"After this G. again passed to the question why we could not tell the story of our lives. And with this the miracle began."p.262
