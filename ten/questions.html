The State Mecca Bingo Players Questions
for Camden Arts Center project with Anna Best

  1. How long have you been playing bingo?
  2. when and how often do you play?
  3. do you play bingo to win, and/or to have a social time, or both?
  4. Do you do the lottery? play the fruit machines/bandits, bet on the horses etc.?
  5. Why is bingo different from these?
  6. what's the most you've won?
  7. What did you spend your winnings on?
  8. How many books do you play at a time?
  9. Do you trust the random number generator more or less than the ball machine?
  10. what do you think of the callers?
  11. Do you have any lucky numbers, colors of pen, place to sit, or any good-luck charms or habits?
  12. Would you describe yourself as superstitious?
  13. What would you say you believe in, are you religious, do you believe in God?
  14. If you could have one MIRACLE what would it be?
    (if it's to win money what woul you spend it on? describe an image)
  15. have you ever dreamt about winning or bingo in your sleep?
  16. What do you think of the State, the decor, environment, building and atmosphere?
