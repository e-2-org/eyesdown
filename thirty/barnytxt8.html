Things aren't always smooth, old ways of doing things don't dissolve over night and finding ways for measuring the success of these projects is far from easy. Importantly though, in spite of the fact that the whys and wherefores of Public Art may at times differ from policy makers to artists, the results of these projects and others like them around Britain can't help but stand up to the toughest scrutiny. 'Miraculous' or not, they are at the forefront of providing genuine, unsanitised, 'Real Life' exchanges between working artists and members of the general public.

Barny Drabble is a freelance curator and writer based in London. He worked with Ross Sinclair and The Agency gallery, curating the event 'I love Real Life' at Ridley Rd market. The author would like to thank Anna Best, Peter Cross, Joanne Rogers & Ross Sinclair.
