In Search Of Real Life and Miracles

A text for 'Engage 7' 1999
by Barny Drabble

'I'm interested in making connections with a general public as an individual living in a city... looking at that thing of being at once on your own and also part of a community or group.'
Anna Best

'I use myself as a symbol of how one individual operates within society or the world. Like tagging an elephant in the wild or something.'
Ross Sinclair

Two recent art events illustrate well how the practices of certain artists are challenging a whole range of assumptions surrounding the ever controversial process of introducing contemporary art to new audiences. Performance and process-based work are at present making new demands on the institutions, galleries and funding structures that choose to support them. When these structures are flexible enough the events that arise are providing unique experiences for the audiences who come in contact with them.
